MUNICIPAL CORPORATION CHANDIGARH (MCC) - PUBLIC HEALTH WING (WATER SUPPLY & SEWERAGE):
- Water Billing Disputes: A citizen may request water meter re-verification or dispute an inflated bill within 30 days of bill receipt via e-Sampark centers or online at mcchandigarh.gov.in. Disputed water bills are verified and re-metered within 15 working days. No testing fee is charged if the meter is found defective (+/- 3% error).
- Pipeline Leakage & Contamination: Water main burst or major pipeline leaks must be inspected within 4 hours and repaired within 24 to 48 hours. Low water pressure complaints are rectified within 48 hours.
- Sewerage Blockage & Overflow: Blockage in main sewer lines or road gullies must be cleared with super-suction machines within 24 hours of complaint registration.
- Helplines: Water Supply Complaint Cell 0172-2540200, MCC ICCC Central Helpline 0172-2787200, e-Sampark Toll-Free 1800-180-1725.

CHANDIGARH POWER DISTRIBUTION LIMITED (CPDL) / ELECTRICITY DEPARTMENT UT CHANDIGARH:
- Power Outages & Breakdowns: Supply interruptions in urban sectors must be restored within 4 hours. In case of 11kV feeder tripping or local distribution transformer burnout, replacement and supply normalization must be completed within 24 to 72 hours.
- Defective or Burnt Electricity Meters: On receiving a complaint via 19121 or online portal, site inspection must be conducted within 7 working days. Defective or burnt meters must be replaced within 15 working days.
- Escalation Matrix: Unresolved outages exceeding 2 hours escalate to the Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO). Outages exceeding 4 hours escalate to the Executive Engineer (XEN). Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum (CGRF) operates at Sector 18, Chandigarh.
- Helplines: 24x7 Electricity Call Centre 19121, Online grievance portal chandigarhpower.com.

MUNICIPAL CORPORATION CHANDIGARH (MCC) - MEDICAL OFFICER OF HEALTH (MOH) — SANITATION & WASTE MANAGEMENT:
- Door-to-Door Waste Collection: Under Swachh Bharat & Chandigarh Municipal Solid Waste Rules, missing segregated door-to-door garbage collection vehicle must be resolved within 24 hours.
- Open Garbage Dumps & Dead Animals: Street garbage piles, overflowing Sehaj Safai Kendras (SSK), or carcass removal complaints must be cleared within 12 to 24 hours.
- Dadumajra & Sector Waste Concerns: Complaints regarding waste processing at Dadumajra plant or storm-water drain choking must be addressed within 48 hours.
- Helplines: Dedicated MCC WhatsApp Grievance Hotline 9915762917, MCC ICCC 0172-2787200.

MUNICIPAL CORPORATION CHANDIGARH (MCC) - BUILDINGS & ROADS (B&R) — STREETLIGHTS & ROADS:
- Streetlight Maintenance: Defective, flickering, or non-functional LED streetlights on V3, V4, V5, and V6 sector roads or dark spots must be repaired within 3 to 7 working days under the Energy Efficiency Services Limited (EESL) maintenance contract.
- Potholes & Road Repair: Dangerous potholes, cave-ins, or broken footpaths on municipal sector roads must be repaired with bitumen patchwork within 7 working days.
- Tree Branch Pruning: Overgrown trees obstructing streetlights or road signages must be pruned by the horticulture division within 5 working days.
- Helplines: MCC ICCC 0172-2787200, e-Sampark 1800-180-1725.

PUNJAB RIGHT TO SERVICE (RTS) ACT AS APPLICABLE TO UT CHANDIGARH:
- Citizens are legally entitled to public services within notified statutory timelines. Failure by designated officers to provide services within timelines attracts penalty under the Chandigarh Right to Service Commission.
